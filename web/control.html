Every pitch adds to the pitcher's count. A third strike or out moves to the next batter; the third out switches the half inning. Hits move every runner up as many bases as the batter and score anyone who reaches home; tap the bases to fix runners. Next batter fills in from the lineup on the Scoring tab.
Hot buttons keys 1-9, 0; Shift for 11-20
Up to 20 hot buttons, shared by every control panel on this server. Click a result to add or remove it.
Player picker (spotlight, cards, bars, lower thirds)
With "Use picked player" on, every player graphic (spotlight, stat cards and bars, tall cards, lower thirds, feature card) shows the player you pick instead of the current batter / pitcher. Turn it off to go back to live. Pitchers show pitching stats on the pitcher graphics; hitters show batting stats.
Talent (announcers on the Talent lower third)
The Talent graphic in every style shows these names (in the Podcast style it shows persons 1 and 2 from the PODCAST tab). Leave Talent 2 blank to show one person.
Bottom ticker
Standings come from the Manual scoring card. The ticker can stay on air with any other graphic.
League & broadcaster logos
The league logo appears on standings, the ticker and show title cards. The broadcaster logo is your network or stream mark (not a team): transitions, bumpers, show graphics, side panels and the Tonight card use it. It never falls back to the home team, so the home logo is not shown twice; with nothing set, those spots stay empty.
Graphics click to take in or out
Editing set: click tiles to add or remove them (editing a style set keeps your version; Restore style sets undoes it). Scorebugs, batter and pitcher lower thirds and the ticker allow one each.
Notes & stats (stat notes, feature card, HR distance)
The stat note replaces the default line on the note-style lower thirds while it has text; clear it after use. HR distance / exit velocity / pitch speed (Classic scorebug info rows) come from these fields. Clear them before the next home run.
Split stat card
Blank rows show the current (or picked) batter's season next to tonight: at-bats, hits, home runs, RBI, average.
Presets (your own graphics from layouts)
Each preset is its own take button (Graphics panel > Your presets). Text can use data tokens such as {away.name}, {home.abbr} or {venue}. Lines: one per line, columns split with | . Restyle a layout in the editor (Layout: ...) and every preset made from it follows.
Tonight's schedule, series probables, road ahead
Probables photos come from the rosters by name. Road ahead logos come from media/logos named after the opponent you type (e.g. YRK.png); otherwise the abbreviation shows.
Umpires
Feeds the Umpires card. One umpire per line.
Colors (every graphic in a style)
Every color the style uses is listed, most used first. Pick a new one and it changes everywhere that color appears in that style (panels, gradients, text, borders, shadows, tables), keeping its transparency. Team colors are in Scoring > Teams, broadcaster colors in League & broadcaster logos, and a single element can be changed in the Editor.
Show (batting practice / pregame / postgame)
The QR code is generated from the link as you type and scans on screen. Leave the link blank to show the "show" sponsor (or brand logo) in that spot. Rows with no value are hidden.
Title cards use the theme logo / name (editor) or the home team's. Show graphics take sponsors from the "show" slot.
