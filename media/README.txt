Drop files here and they are picked up automatically (no restart needed):

photos/       Player headshots or cutouts (transparent PNG works best). You can also upload
              them in the control panel: Players & headshots.
              Name the file after the player: alex-rivera.png
              or team abbreviation + number: si-24.png
logos/        Team logos named by abbreviation or team name: si.png, li.png,
              harbor-city-gulls.png. A logo URL set in the control panel wins.
backgrounds/  Full-screen team backgrounds for the Matchup graphic: si.jpg, li.jpg
videos/       Opener / transition videos. Use WebM (VP9) with alpha for transparency.
              Reference one in the editor as /media/videos/opener.webm, or set the
              theme's openerVideo value.

Names are matched ignoring case, spaces and punctuation.
sponsors/     Sponsor logos (transparent PNG or SVG read best). Add them to the
              sponsor library in the control panel's Sponsors card, or upload
              straight from that card.
